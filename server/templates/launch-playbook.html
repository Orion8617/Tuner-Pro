Week 1 — Foundation & Soft Launch Days 1–7

ASO Setup

Submit app to App Store and Google Play. Optimize title: "GuitarTune: Guitar Tuner — No Ads." Write keyword-rich subtitle (30 chars iOS): "Accurate Tuner, Zero Ads." Set category to Music, add all 100 keyword characters on iOS. Localize listing in Spanish for Mexico/Spain.

SEO Indexing

Submit sitemap.xml to Google Search Console. Request indexing for /compare, /tunings, and all 8 /vs/ pages. Verify canonical URLs resolve correctly. Add site to Bing Webmaster Tools.

Meta Ads — Campaign Setup

Create Meta Business account. Install Meta Pixel on landing page. Set up App Install campaign with 3 ad sets (Angle A/B/C). Targeting: ages 18–35, interests: guitar, acoustic guitar, electric guitar, music production. Lookalike audience from email list (if available) or landing page visitors. Start at $20/day across all ad sets.

Google RSA — Campaign Setup

Create Search campaign with 15 headlines and 5 descriptions from /ad-copy page. Ad groups: "guitar tuner app" (broad match), "guitartuna alternative" (exact match), "no ads guitar tuner" (phrase match). Set max CPC at $1.50. Enable all extensions: sitelink, callout, structured snippet.

Organic Social

Create @guitartune accounts on Instagram, TikTok, and X/Twitter. Post 1 demo video showing real-time tuning (screen recording of the app). Pin "No ads. No bloat. Just tuning." intro post.

Review Seeding

Share beta with 10–15 guitarist friends/contacts. Ask for honest App Store reviews. Respond to every review within 24 hours. Target: 10+ reviews by end of Week 1.

Week 1 KPIs

500 installs 10+ reviews Sitemap submitted 3 ad sets live CPI < $4.00
Week 2 — Paid Optimization & Content Days 8–14

Meta Ads — Optimize

Review ad set performance. Kill the worst-performing angle (highest CPI). Double budget on the winning angle. Add 1 new creative variation of the winner (different headline, same visual). Test feed vs. Stories placement split.

Google Ads — Refine Keywords

Review search terms report. Add negative keywords for irrelevant queries (e.g., "guitar tuner hardware," "clip-on tuner"). Increase bids on high-converting terms. Pause low-CTR headlines from RSA rotation.

TikTok Spark Ads — Launch

Film 3 TikTok scripts from /ad-copy page (or hire a creator on Fiverr for $50–100 each). Post organically first, then boost top performer as Spark Ad. Targeting: 18–30, interests: guitar, music, learn guitar. Start at $15/day.

Content Marketing

Publish 3 blog-style posts on social: "How to tune to Drop D in 10 seconds," "Open G tuning — Keith Richards' secret," "Why your tuner app shows you ads." Cross-post to Reddit r/guitar, r/guitarlessons (as helpful, non-promotional posts).

SEO Monitoring

Check Google Search Console for indexed pages. Identify any pages stuck in "Discovered — not indexed." Improve internal linking from /compare hub to underperforming /vs/ pages. Verify all 15 /tunings/ pages are crawled.

Review Loop

Implement in-app review prompt (expo-store-review) triggered after 3 successful tuning sessions. Respond to all new reviews. Monitor 1-star reviews for bug reports — fix and ship same day if possible.

Week 2 KPIs

1,500 cumulative installs CPI < $3.00 20+ reviews TikTok live 5+ pages indexed
Week 3 — Scale Winners & Retention Days 15–21

Paid Scaling

Increase Meta budget to $30/day on winning ad set. Scale Google Ads budget 25% on converting keywords. On TikTok, boost the best-performing Spark Ad to $25/day. Total daily spend: ~$60–70/day.

Retargeting Setup

Create Meta retargeting audience: users who visited landing page but didn't install. Create retargeting ad with Angle A (pain point) + "Still tuning with ads?" messaging. Set frequency cap at 3 impressions per user per week.

Premium Conversion Push

A/B test premium upsell screen: current layout vs. simplified "one big button" layout. Add limited-time 20% discount banner for annual plan ($7.99 instead of $9.99). Track conversion rate from free → paid with analytics events.

Community Building

Post weekly "Tuning of the Week" on social — highlight one alternate tuning with a famous song example. Engage with guitar communities on Reddit, Discord (Guitar, Music Production servers). Share user testimonials from reviews.

ASO Iteration

Review keyword rankings in AppFollow/Sensor Tower. Update screenshots if current ones underperform (test dark screenshots vs. light). Add "What's New" text highlighting the latest update. A/B test app icon if store supports it.

Bilingual Expansion

Run Spanish-language Meta ads targeting Mexico, Spain, Argentina. Use translated ad copy from i18n system. Test one Spanish TikTok script. Monitor CPI — Spanish-language markets often have 30–50% lower CPI.

Week 3 KPIs

3,000 cumulative installs CPI < $2.50 30+ reviews 25 paid conversions 10+ pages indexed
Week 4 — Sustain & Iterate Days 22–30

Channel Performance Review

Rank all channels by CPI and 7-day retention. Cut any channel with CPI > $4.00 and retention < 20%. Reallocate budget to best-performing channel. Document learnings for Month 2 strategy.

SEO Content Expansion

Identify top 5 organic keywords driving traffic in Search Console. Create 3 new /tunings/ pages for trending search terms (e.g., Nashville tuning, Celtic tuning, Baritone tuning). Update sitemap and request indexing.

Influencer Outreach (Micro)

Identify 5–10 guitar micro-influencers on TikTok/Instagram (5K–50K followers). Offer free lifetime premium access in exchange for an honest review video. No script required — authentic reviews perform best.

Retention & Engagement

Analyze Day 1, Day 3, Day 7 retention rates. If D7 retention is below 25%, investigate: is the tuner accurate enough? Are users finding alternate tunings? Add push notification for "Tuning Tip of the Week" (optional opt-in).

Revenue Optimization

Review free-to-paid conversion funnel. If conversion is below 2%, test: moving the paywall trigger earlier (after 2 tuning sessions instead of 5), highlighting price comparison with competitors, offering 7-day free trial of premium.

Month 2 Planning

Compile 30-day report: total installs, CPI by channel, conversion rate, revenue, top SEO pages, top-performing ad creative. Set Month 2 goals (10K installs, $500 MRR target). Plan blog content calendar (8 posts/month).

Week 4 KPIs

5,000 cumulative installs CPI < $2.50 4.5★ rating 50 paid conversions 20+ pages indexed Month 2 plan ready
